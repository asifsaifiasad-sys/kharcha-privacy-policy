Privacy Policy – Kharcha: Daily Expense Tracker

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Kharcha – Daily Expense Tracker (“the App”) helps users record income, expenses, transactions, and budgets. This policy explains how information is handled.

1. Information stored in the app

Users may enter income, expenses, transaction categories, dates, notes, and budget information. The app is intended to store this information locally on the user's device and does not require an account or login.

2. Collection and sharing

The app is designed to work offline. The developer does not intentionally collect or transmit users' financial records to a developer-operated server or sell personal financial information. This statement must be updated if the released app includes analytics, advertising, cloud services, or other third-party SDKs that collect data.

3. Data security

Information is stored on the device. Users should protect their device with a screen lock and take care when exporting or sharing transaction files. Device backup settings or other software may affect backup and access to local data.

4. Data deletion and retention

Users can delete records using the app's “Clear All Records” feature if available. Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored data, but operating-system backups may retain some data depending on device settings.

5. Children's privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children through developer-operated servers. If the app's audience or data practices change, this section will be reviewed and updated.

6. Third-party services

The app is intended to work without a backend or online account. Any third-party services included in the released version must be disclosed as required by applicable laws and Google Play policies.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL.
